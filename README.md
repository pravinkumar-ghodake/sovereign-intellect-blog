> ## 📖 About This Repository
>
> <div align="justify">
>
> **Sovereign Intellect** is a blog on intellectual sovereignty — how independent researchers own their claims, anchor their provenance, and resist algorithmic erasure in the AI era. It publishes structured essay trilogies on open science, authorship accountability, metric capture, and the evaluation of independent research.
>
> **Purpose:** To provide a modern, machine-readable, SEO-first reading experience for long-form analytical essays on the future of independent science.
>
> **Design Principles:**
>
> - 📖 **Readable first** — every essay is plain Markdown source, versioned and diffable
> - 🔗 **Trilogy-linked** — every essay is part of a numbered series with cross-navigation
> - 🤖 **Machine-readable** — JSON-LD structured data on every page
> - 🔍 **Search-optimized** — sitemap, feed, canonical URLs, structured headings
> - 🌍 **Open access** — prose CC BY 4.0, code MIT
> - 🎨 **Typographically disciplined** — consistent use of `center`, `justify`, badges, tables
>
